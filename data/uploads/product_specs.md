# Product Specifications & Business Rules

## Discount Codes
- The discount code `SAVE15` applies a 15% discount to the cart subtotal.
- Discount is applied before shipping charges.

## Shipping
- Standard shipping is free.
- Express shipping costs $10 (or equivalent in local currency).

## Cart Rules
- Quantity for each item must be a non-negative integer.
- If both quantities are zero, checkout should not proceed.
